How FOLIX works

From a crop image to actionable insight.

FOLIX uses AI vision to examine crop images, identify visible signs of disease, estimate severity, and guide the next step.

The result is an AI-assisted assessment to support your decision. It is not a laboratory diagnosis.

Five steps, from image to action.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Take or upload an image of the crop.

  2. 02

    Analyze

    FOLIX examines the visible plant and symptoms using AI vision.

  3. 03

    Identify

    The AI provides the most likely disease or condition when the visual evidence supports an identification.

  4. 04

    Assess

    The system estimates severity and communicates uncertainty when the image does not provide enough evidence.

  5. 05

    Act

    You receive recommended next steps, and can save or forward the case when appropriate.

What FOLIX provides.

A scan is the starting point. These are the tools that help you use it.

  • AI crop analysis

    Analyze crop images with AI vision.

  • Severity monitoring

    Track the severity of detected crop problems over time.

  • Scan history

    Keep previous assessments available for reference.

  • Disease map

    View aggregated regional disease activity.

  • Agricultural network

    Connect with relevant agricultural support.

  • Case forwarding

    Forward serious cases to appropriate verified agricultural contacts when you choose to.

Not every image tells the whole story.

If the visible evidence is unclear, FOLIX is designed to say so, instead of presenting an unsupported diagnosis as fact.
